Our Story

An absolute gem
in Donegal Town

Mo Chlann is a warm, welcoming space in the heart of Donegal Town — where exceptional specialty coffee meets an intimate wine and cocktail bar. Impeccable interiors, genuinely lovely staff, and a menu made with care.

Whether you stop in for a morning flat white, an afternoon chai, or an evening glass of wine with friends — this is your place. Stylish yet cosy. Always smiling.
